# Product brief

ChapterPrep helps A2–C1 language learners prepare focused, contextual vocabulary for the chapter they are about to read. Reading flow and learner control are primary. See [CHAPTERPREP_PRD.md](CHAPTERPREP_PRD.md) for full requirements.

## Current slice

Create a book, select it, paste a chapter, and reload it from local storage. The UI reports extraction as not configured rather than implying AI support.

## Boundaries

Pasted text is first-class. TXT/EPUB/PDF parsing, real AI extraction, accounts, synchronization, reader annotations, preparation mode, export, and progress are later milestones.
